# 📖 Project Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the **Zomato Restaurant Dataset** using Python. The objective is to analyze customer preferences, restaurant categories, ratings, spending patterns, online ordering behavior, and customer engagement to generate meaningful business insights.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow including:

- Data Cleaning
- Data Preprocessing
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Data Visualization
- Business Insights
- Business Recommendations

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Restaurant businesses and food delivery platforms need to understand customer preferences and ordering behavior to improve customer satisfaction, optimize services, and increase business growth.

This analysis helps identify trends that can support better business decisions using historical restaurant data.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand the restaurant dataset
- Clean and preprocess raw data
- Analyze restaurant categories
- Study customer ratings
- Analyze customer voting behavior
- Explore customer spending patterns
- Compare online and offline ordering
- Generate business insights and recommendations

# 📊 Business Questions

The project answers the following business questions:

1. What type of restaurant do the majority of customers prefer?
2. Which restaurant type has received the highest number of customer votes?
3. What ratings have the majority of restaurants received?
4. What is the average spending for two people?
5. Which ordering mode (Online or Offline) has received the higher average customer rating?
6. Which type of restaurant receives more offline orders?

# 💡 Key Findings

- Dining restaurants are the most preferred restaurant category among customers.
- Dining restaurants received the highest customer votes, indicating strong engagement.
- Most restaurants have ratings in the higher range, reflecting generally positive customer satisfaction.
- The average cost for two people falls within a moderate price range.
- Online ordering received higher average customer ratings compared to offline ordering.
- Dining restaurants showed the highest number of offline orders.

> **Note:** Update these findings if your final analysis produces different results.

---

# 💼 Business Recommendations

- Increase promotional campaigns for high-performing restaurant categories.
- Improve visibility for lower-engagement restaurant types.
- Encourage restaurants with lower ratings to enhance service quality.
- Optimize pricing strategies based on customer spending patterns.
- Strengthen online ordering services to improve customer satisfaction.
- Launch targeted offers for restaurant categories with strong offline demand.
